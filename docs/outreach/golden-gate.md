## 2. ALERTCalifornia, UC San Diego (Sutro Tower 1/2, Mt Tam East)

- **Contact:** the contact form at <https://alertcalifornia.org/> (terms at <https://alertcalifornia.org/terms/>).
- **Why permission is needed:** the images are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, which forbids sharing adapted material. Derived renders would need an exception.
- **Also note:** these are pan-tilt-zoom cameras. A fixed preset would be needed, or frames are kept only when the reported azimuth and zoom match.

> **Collection:** at most 4 frames per hour, recorded only when the camera's reported azimuth and zoom match a fixed view.
>
> **Requests:**
> 1. Permission for this automated retrieval.
> 2. An exception to the NoDerivatives clause, so that I may publish simulated renders and small crops with the credit "ALERTCalifornia | UC San Diego".
>
> I'll remove anything on request within 7 days. Thank you.
>
> [Your name], Lookout project, [project contact address]
